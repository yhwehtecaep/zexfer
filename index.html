FAQs

Quick Answers for Smart Trading

Find quick answers to common questions about our platform and how to get started.

What exchanges are supported?

We currently support Binance and Bybit for automated trading. We are working on adding OKX and KuCoin in the near future.

Are my funds secure when connecting to my exchange?

Yes. We use API keys that only have 'Trade' permissions. We never ask for or require 'Withdrawal' permissions, meaning your funds can never leave your exchange account.

Can I test the platform without risking real money?

Absolutely. We offer a full Paper Trading mode during your 7-day free trial so you can see how the automation performs with virtual funds.
